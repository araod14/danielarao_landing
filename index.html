Before we start

Can you scrape any website?

I first review the source, the data you need and your access permissions. Feasibility and permitted use depend on the site and the project; I do not promise that every source is suitable. Any legal questions specific to your use should be resolved before work starts.

What happens if the site changes?

Retries handle temporary failures; site changes can require code changes. I include failure alerts in deployed workflows and agree the support period and scope with you upfront. Ongoing maintenance can be scoped separately.

Do you charge by the project or by the hour?

Either, depending on how clearly we can define the work. A defined scope suits a project price; exploratory or changing work can suit hourly billing. We agree the arrangement before starting.

How will I receive the data?

CSV, a database, an API or Google Sheets—whichever fits your workflow. We agree the fields, format, frequency and deployment needs before the build.

Can we work under an NDA?

Send your requirements before sharing confidential material. We can review the NDA and agree confidentiality terms as part of the project setup.

Can we work in English?

Yes. I work with international clients in English, with fluent technical reading and writing and conversational spoken English. Spanish is my native language. I am based in Venezuela, UTC−4.

Tell me what needs to work.

Send the site or system, the data you need, and what you want to do with it. I reply in less than 24 hours.
